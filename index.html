Discover the Joy of Farm Life

Harvest Moon: Home Sweet Home offers a rich farming experience that goes beyond planting and harvesting. You’ll manage your land, care for animals, and explore the changing seasons, each bringing its own challenges and rewards.

Watch your farm grow from humble beginnings to a bustling homestead that reflects your style and dedication. Beyond farming, the game encourages you to deepen connections with the local villagers. Celebrate festivals, exchange stories, and find friendship in a charming countryside setting.
